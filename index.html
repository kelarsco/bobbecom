About me

Who’s Behind Ecomriel

I’m the founder and creative lead of Ecomriel, an eCommerce-first agency focused on Branding, Conversion-Driven UI/UX, and High-Performing Store Development.

I started solo, but quickly realized a one-person setup limits how far you can scale results, especially in eCommerce where strategy, CRO, UX, and brand need to work together as one system. So I expanded, building a team of elite operators and designers to take our execution & performance to a far higher level.

I’m still fully involved in every project, leading direction, shaping strategy, and diving into the actual design work — because that’s where the biggest revenue impact begins.
